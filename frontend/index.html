Scientific Data Sources & Provenance

Government Sensor Networks, Telemetry & Geological Repositories

Safety Scoring & Weight Formula (Score 0 to 100)
100 = Maximum safety rating with optimal road clearance
Points are subtracted according to multi-hazard risk indices:
  • 25% Landslides & Mudslides: ISRO Landslide Atlas of India & GSI National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM).
  • 25% Heavy Rainfall & Cloudbursts: IMD (India Meteorological Dept) AWS automated stations & Cherrapunji Doppler Radar.
  • 20% River Flooding & Waterlogging: Central Water Commission (CWC) Brahmaputra/Barak basin hydrological discharge gauges.
  • 15% Vertical Slope & Incline: NASA SRTM 30m Digital Elevation Model & ISRO CartoDEM; graded by Indian Roads Congress (IRC:SP:48).
  • 15% Highway Incident Bulletins: Border Roads Organisation (BRO Projects Vartak/Sevark/Pushpak) & State Police Traffic Feeds.
Authoritative Data Repositories Where Data Is Taken:
ISRO Bhuvan Geoportal & NRSC 80,000+ mapped landslides across the Himalayas and NE Region with radar interferometry.
Geological Survey of India (GSI) 1:50,000 NLSM high-resolution macro-scale slope susceptibility polygons and fault lines.
NASA SRTM & ISRO CartoDEM 30-meter continuous digital elevation model for calculating exact road incline gradients.
Indian Roads Congress (IRC) IRC:SP:48 Mountain Road Manual and IRC Class 70R bridge tonnage guidelines.
Central Water Commission (CWC) River water level gauges, flash flood hydrographs, and bridge submergence telemetry.
Border Roads Organisation (BRO) Daily mountain pass transit bulletins, snow-clearing reports, and axle load clearances.
